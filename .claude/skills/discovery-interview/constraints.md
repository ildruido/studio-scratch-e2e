# Branch: constraints and non-goals

Builds on: the product foundation and the Discovery profile's Domain risks row.

## Outline

- **Budget:** what can be spent to build and to run it, per month.
- **People and time:** who works on it, how much, and any date that matters.
- **Legal and domain:** each domain risk from the profile, and what it rules out or requires.
- **Platform:** where it must run, and what it must work with.
- **Running costs:** the costs that grow with use (AI calls, storage, messages, hosting), with a ceiling per user or per month if there is one.
- **Non-goals:** what the product will deliberately not do, each with the reason.
- **Assumptions** and **Open questions.**

## Rules this document adds

- A constraint is a number or a clear yes or no wherever possible ("under 20 a month to run", not "cheap").
- Ask for the non-goals explicitly: what would the App Creator say no to even if users asked? At least three.
- A legal or cost point you cannot settle from the documents is an open question for a study (`research` skill), not a guess.
- If the interview surfaces a new domain risk, update the profile's Domain risks row (`discovery-profile set`).
