# Test script: Tip Splitter v0.1.0

Open the Tip Splitter page (the private Claude artifact link that comes with the release). The small version label near the title should read v0.1.0. No permission prompt appears in this version.

## TS-001 Split a bill with a tip

1. Do: Type 100 in the Bill box, tap the 20% button, and set People to 4.
   Expect: The page says "Each person pays 30.00" and shows "Bill 100.00 + tip 20.00 = total 120.00".
2. Do: Change the Bill to 50, tap 18%, and set People to 3.
   Expect: "Each person pays 19.67" (rounded up, never down).
3. Do: Type 10 in the Custom % box, set the Bill to 100 and People to 2.
   Expect: "Each person pays 55.00", and none of the 15%, 18% or 20% buttons looks selected.
4. Do: Tap 15%.
   Expect: The Custom % box empties, 15% looks selected, and the share becomes 57.50.
5. Do: Set People to 0.
   Expect: The share disappears and a short hint says to set at least 1 person.
6. Do: Empty the Bill box.
   Expect: A hint asks you to enter the bill, and no share is shown.
7. Do: Open the page on your phone, or make the window narrow.
   Expect: Everything stays on screen with no sideways scrolling.

Reply with the step numbers that failed and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
